About RescueNet

Our Vision

To create a disaster-resilient India where no life is lost due to a lack of coordination or delayed response. We aim to integrate technology and human courage into a single "Rescue Net" that covers every corner of the nation.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide rapid, organized, and technical relief during natural and man-made disasters. We coordinate between government bodies, local volunteers, and specialized rescue units to ensure aid reaches the most vulnerable within the "Golden Hour."
